6. Gaps and Earth use

What is missing

There is currently no partial-gravity combustion data in PSI. Partial gravity is what a Moon base would have. Microgravity and normal-gravity results could bound what happens at intermediate gravity levels.

Source: challenge page

Applications on Earth

  • Fine-water-mist extinguishers, developed for use on the ISS
  • Smoke detection improvements
  • Firefighter gear, including protective clothing and air tanks

Source: challenge page

7. Key finding

In Saffire VI, smoke was readily detected at 981 seconds, after most of the hazardous gas release had already happened.
Source: Saffire VI presentation, ICES 2024 (preliminary)
